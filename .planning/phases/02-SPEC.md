# MediTrace OS - Phase 2 Specification: MediTrace Logistics & CBWTF Handover Portal

## 1. Overview & Objective
**MediTrace Logistics** extends MediTrace OS by bridging hospital bedside waste collection with the **Common Bio-medical Waste Treatment Facility (CBWTF)** transportation network. It validates the digital chain of custody under BMWM Rules 2016 by allowing vehicle operators to scan ward Form-IV QR codes, reconcile weighbridge payloads, track transport fleet routes, and issue digital handover certificates.

## 3. Detailed Component Specifications

### 3.1 Route `/logistics` & Component `components/CBWTFHandoverPortal.tsx`
- **QR Code Scanner**: Decodes bedside manifest JSON payloads containing `manifestId`, `facility`, `timestamp`, `breakdown`, and `cbwtfHash`.
- **Weight Reconciliation Engine**:
  - Compares Bedside Weight ($\text{kg}$) vs Truck Weighbridge Scale ($\text{kg}$).
  - Calculates $\Delta = |\text{Truck Weight} - \text{Bedside Weight}|$.
  - Flags **DISCREPANCY ALERT** if $\Delta > 0.5\text{kg}$ or $>2\%$ variance.
- **GPS Fleet Tracking Canvas**:
  - Simulated vehicle route map with animated GPS truck icon moving between hospital ward dock and central incineration plant.
  - Live ETA timer, vehicle plate number (`KA-01-MW-9401`), and ambient compartment temperature.
- **Chain-of-Custody Sign-off**:
  - Interactive signature canvas / verification check.
  - Generates official CBWTF Transfer Receipt modal.
